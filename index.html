🎓 Welcome to Farasi Lane Senior School 🎓

Message From Principal

Farasi Lane High School was established in 2017 with a vision to provide quality education and nurture well-rounded individuals prepared for the future. Since its inception, the school has grown into a reputable mixed day secondary school located in Westlands Sub-County, Nairobi County, along Lower Kabete Road in Kitisuru Ward.

Our school is set in a beautiful, serene environment that provides a calm and safe atmosphere conducive for effective learning and personal growth. We believe that a peaceful environment plays a key role in shaping disciplined, focused, and successful learners.

At Farasi Lane High School, we pride ourselves on having a team of highly qualified and well-trained teachers who are dedicated to academic excellence and student mentorship. Our staff work tirelessly to ensure that every learner reaches their full potential both academically and socially.

As a Christian-based institution, we uphold strong moral values, guiding our students to develop integrity, discipline, and respect in all aspects of life.

We are also a center for talent nurturing, offering a wide range of co-curricular activities including sports such as football (soccer), chess, and other engaging programs that help students discover and develop their talents.

About Us

Farasi Lane Senior School is a world-class institution dedicated to excellence in education. We foster a supportive environment where students discover their potential, develop critical thinking, and grow into responsible global citizens.

Academics

We offer a wide range of subjects focusing on 8:4:4, CBE, and extra-curricular activities. Our curriculum nurtures curiosity, creativity, and achievement while maintaining high academic standards.

Admissions

Farasi Lane Senior School offers a seamless admission process designed to identify and nurture future leaders. We admit students who demonstrate academic potential, discipline, and a willingness to grow.
