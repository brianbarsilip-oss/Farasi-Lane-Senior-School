Message From Principal

Farasi Lane High School was established in 2017 with a vision to provide quality education and nurture well-rounded individuals prepared for the future. Since its inception, the school has grown into a reputable mixed day secondary school located in Westlands Sub-County, Nairobi County, along Lower Kabete Road in Kitisuru Ward.

Our school is set in a beautiful, serene environment that provides a calm and safe atmosphere conducive for effective learning and personal growth. We believe that a peaceful environment plays a key role in shaping disciplined, focused, and successful learners.

At Farasi Lane High School, we pride ourselves on having a team of highly qualified and well-trained teachers who are dedicated to academic excellence and student mentorship. Our staff work tirelessly to ensure that every learner reaches their full potential both academically and socially.

As a Christian-based institution, we uphold strong moral values, guiding our students to develop integrity, discipline, and respect in all aspects of life.

We are also a center for talent nurturing, offering a wide range of co-curricular activities including sports such as football (soccer), chess, and other engaging programs that help students discover and develop their talents.

Farasi Lane Secondary & Senior School

Who We Are

Farasi Lane Secondary and Senior School is a progressive institution established in 2017, located in Kitisuru Ward along Lower Kabete Road in Westlands Sub-County, Nairobi County. We provide a mixed day learning environment and are committed to academic excellence, discipline, and holistic development of our learners.

Our Mission

Dedicated To Excellence in providing quality, holistic, and value-based education that nurtures academic excellence, integrity, and leadership in every learner.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution recognized for excellence in education, innovation, and character development.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Excellence
  • Discipline
  • Responsibility
  • Respect
  • Innovation

Our Environment

Our school is set in a beautiful, serene, and secure environment that is highly conducive to learning. The peaceful surroundings allow students to focus and achieve their full academic potential.

Our Staff

Our team consists of highly qualified, experienced, and dedicated teachers who are passionate about guiding students academically, morally, and socially.

Christian Foundation

Farasi Lane is a Christian-based school that promotes strong moral values, spiritual growth, and ethical behavior among learners.

Holistic Development

We encourage participation in co-curricular activities such as sports, music, drama, leadership programs, and community engagement to ensure well-rounded development.

Why Choose Us?

Our Academic Program

At Farasi Lane Secondary and Senior School, we offer a comprehensive and well-structured academic program aligned with the national curriculum. Our focus is to equip learners with knowledge, critical thinking skills, and competencies necessary for success in higher education and the modern world.

Curriculum
